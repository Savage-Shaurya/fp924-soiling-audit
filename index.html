Plant FP-924 (2,952 kWp, 35 inverters, 25 blocks). Data: Solar Link daily inverter energy 3-Jul-2025 to 22-Nov-2026, 5-min power 22-Oct to 22-Nov-2026, SMP3 pyranometer, ERA5 weather, CAMS PM10. Economics year: 2025-11-23 to 2026-11-22 (measured meter energy 4,210,888 kWh).

Verdict. The current ₹3.65 lakh/yr net figure is too high: it sits at the upper bound. The 0.258 %/day rate comes from a step detector that the data do not support (output does not decay again after detected cleanings: -0.003 %/day vs −0.215 %/day expected). Best defensible estimate: ≈₹0.91 L/yr net (range ₹0 to ₹5.54 L), cleaning about every 31 dry days.

7.2 Corrected equations (only where a number changes)

Daily energy E: annual mean 11,537 kWh/d (measured), not a November forecast (~9,200 kWh/d).
T* = √(2C / (p·E·r)) = √(2 × 29,040 / (7.75 × 11,537 × 0.0010)) = 25 d (B; simulation with rain gives 31 d).
Daily soiling: s(d+1) = s(d) + r on dry days; rain ≥ 5 mm: s × 0.1; cleaning: s × (1 − η).
Recoverable energy = Σ E(d)·s_rain-only(d)·f_clip − Σ E(d)·s_with-cleaning(d)·f_clip = 72,391 kWh − 45,659 kWh = 26,732 kWh (B).
Net = 7.75 × 26,732 − 4 × 29,040 = ₹2,07,171 − ₹1,16,160 = ₹91,011 (B).
Rate evidence: r = rain jump ÷ dry days = 2.34 % ÷ 21 d = 0.11 %/d (B); 2.16 % ÷ 42 d = 0.05 %/d (A).

A4. Method notes

  • Inverter columns matched by position (portal repeats 'Inverter8', 'Inverter9'). _corrected 5-min file used for 1-Nov; '(1)' duplicates skipped.
  • Quality flags per inverter-day: missing/zero, logger dump (>7.2 kWh/kWp and the day before), cloudy (expected <1.5 kWh/kWp), outage (<50 % of campus median).
  • Expected energy: ERA5 hourly GHI/DNI/DHI, isotropic transposition, NOCT cell temperature, γ = −0.29 %/°C, DC derate 0.97, inverter 97.5 %, clipped at AC rating.
  • Economics: day-by-day over 23-Nov-2025 to 22-Nov-2026 with real ERA5 rain; policy = clean when T days have passed since the last cleaning or ≥5 mm rain.
  • Block files thapar_panels.csv and thapar_block_sensors_hourly.csv contain modelled per-panel and sensor values (no such measurements exist in the portal); they were not used.
  • Re-run everything: audit/.venv/bin/python audit/scripts/run_all.py.
